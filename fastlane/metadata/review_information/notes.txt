1Wordon requests no permissions. A cold launch shows no permission prompt, and there is no usage-description string because no permission is declared.

There is no login, no in-app purchase, no shared user-generated content, no account-deletion flow, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt. Goal names you type stay on this device. "Reset all data" wipes this device only. There is no account to delete.

1Wordon is a dark-screen savings notebook for someone who wants to set aside loose cash by hand. You name a goal, record cash drops into a waiting tray, then commit the whole tray so a vine grows toward that goal. Nothing is linked to a bank.

No credentials or demo account are needed. A cold launch shows a dark screen with a gray placeholder block and no words. If loading lasts, a spinner appears under that block. The first time, three intro pages follow. "Skip" is at the top of every page and finishes the intro immediately. The first page shows "Cash, by hand." and "Name a goal. Drop loose cash. Nothing is linked to a bank." with "Next". The second shows "The vine waits." and "A drop does not grow the vine. Set the batch and the height moves." with "Next". The third shows "Payday is the commit." and "Unpooled cash stays put until you set it. Peel takes back only the last drop." with "Continue". "Continue" or "Skip" opens "Garden". With no goal it says "The garden is empty." and "Plant your first goal." Tap "Plant First Goal". On "Plant Goal", fill "Goals", "Target", and "Currency" (it starts as "USD"), and leave "Vine number" blank or enter a whole number. Tap "Plant Goal". On the garden, enter a positive number in "Amount" and tap "Drop", or tap a quick amount (the built-in chips are 5, 10, 20, and 50). "Set Canopy" commits the tray. "Peel" asks "Peel the last drop?" and then "Peel" removes only the latest uncommitted drop. "History", "Goals", and "Settings" are in the top bar. On "History", "Set Canopy" and "Plant First Goal" only close the sheet; commit and plant from the garden. "Goals" switches the current goal or opens "Plant Goal". In "Settings", "Add amount" changes quick amounts, "Export CSV" shares a text file, "Show intro" replays the three pages, "Contact" opens the support page in the browser, and "Reset all data" asks "Reset the garden?". "Drop" stays disabled until "Amount" is a positive number, and also while the phase is "Ripe". "Set Canopy" stays disabled while the tray is empty or the goal is "Ripe". The Apple Watch app is also named "1Wordon" and always shows a fixed sample: "Rainy week", "Rent buffer", "Train fare", and "Bike lock". "Set Canopy" on the watch is not a button, and the watch face never changes when you edit the phone.

Goals, drops, and commits stay on this device. "Export CSV" can share a text file of the garden if you choose to send it, and "Contact" in Settings opens the support page in the browser.

There is no country lock. Money uses the currency code on the goal, which starts as "USD", with the device's currency formatting. The amount field reads numbers in the device's number format. Dates on the phone use a fixed English form such as "Oct 22, 2026", even if the device language is different. The watch log shows dates as eight-digit numbers.

No licence or regulated-industry paperwork applies. 1Wordon is a personal notebook for cash you record by hand, and nothing is linked to a bank.

The app is a personal log only and gives no recommendations. It shows no health, medical, or product-health information, and it does not scan barcodes or QR codes.
